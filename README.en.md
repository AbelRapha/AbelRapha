# Hi, I'm Abel 👋

Data engineer and analytics professional, focused on AI products. I study Transportation Engineering at UNICAMP and have worked with data since 2021.

- 🏥 I currently work at Hapvida, where I model clinical data and build dashboards that operations and medical audit use to make decisions.
- 🧮 I enjoy operations research problems, such as routing, resource allocation and planning.
- 🛠️ I build tools for real problems, such as [Premius](https://app.usepremius.com), a portfolio management platform for insurance brokers.
- 🎥 I teach programming and data on the [Descomplicando Dados](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCwA0jaKFfgyOUrWx5CN_Nzw) YouTube channel (in Portuguese).

## What I do

- **Data engineering:** pipelines, ETLs and data modeling in Oracle, Databricks and BigQuery
- **BI and analytics:** dashboards in Power BI and Tableau
- **Applied AI:** LLMs, OCR, automation and agents
- **Optimization:** operations research with Python
